## About Us
We pick up surplus food from the dining halls, Panera, and catered events and donate it to our community partner Joseph's House, which provides food to members of the Troy community in need. Since our founding in 2018, we have recovered over 20,000 lbs of food that would otherwise be sent to the dumpsters!
