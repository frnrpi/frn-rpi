RPI Food Recovery Network

We are a club doing what we can to reduce food waste and help make RPI more sustainable by transporting excess food from on campus dining locations to our communtiny Partners. Our chapter was founded in 2018 and we are supported by a national office that helps FRN chapters across the nation reduce food waste nation-wide. Our club currently picks up from Panera, Commons, Sage, and special events, typically ones with catering. We currently donate all our food donations to Joseph's House in downtown Troy. We also host a food drive at the end of every semester and partner with RPI's Food Pantry to collect donations.


Interested in fighting food waste at RPI and serving the Troy community? Join Our Discord where all the communication is happening. Get notified for the events that we host, volunteer opportunities, and sign up to recover food. Throughout the semester we also try to host some workshops and events to learn more about food waste and how we can make a difference in our personal lives and through volunteering.

Captain

Every pickup has a designated Captain, either an E-Board member or a volunteer with a car. They are in charge of driving the food to the destination and completing our Post-Recovery Form to help us log every recovery we do.

Volunteer

Volunteers do not need a car and are there to help the Captain move the food. While Panera only typically gives us 1 box per night, larger donations from Dining Halls or catering events can produce many trays of food that we need a lot of help with to transport it all to our vehicles and partner facilities.

Request a Food Pickup

We are also able to pick up surplus food from catered events! We ask that organizations let us know through our form as early as possible, with a preference of at least 2 weeks in advance. We also require the food to be in a safe condition to donate - for example, items can't be open on a buffet where no food workers could access it. If you have any questions, send an email to frnrpi@gmail.com.
